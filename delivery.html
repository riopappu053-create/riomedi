Support

Delivery FAQs

Common questions about our delivery process and policies.

What are the delivery charges?

We offer FREE standard delivery on all orders above ₹499. For orders below ₹499, a nominal delivery fee of ₹40 applies. Express delivery is available in select areas for an additional ₹49.

Can I return medicines if they are unsealed?

Due to health and safety regulations, we cannot accept returns for unsealed medicines. However, if you receive a damaged or incorrect product, we offer a 100% replacement guarantee within 48 hours.

How do I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an SMS and email with a live tracking link. You can also track your order in real-time from the 'My Orders' section in your account dashboard.

Do you deliver temperature-sensitive medicines?

Yes! We use specialized cold-chain packaging (insulated boxes with gel packs) for temperature-sensitive medications like insulin and vaccines to ensure they remain effective during transit.
