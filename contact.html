FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common queries about our pharmacy services.

How do I upload my prescription?

You can upload your prescription directly on our website by visiting the "Upload Rx" page. Simply take a clear photo or scan of your prescription (JPG, PNG, or PDF) and fill in your details. Our pharmacist will verify and prepare your order within 2 hours.

Do you offer home delivery?

Yes, we offer free home delivery across major cities. Orders placed before 6 PM are delivered the same day. For urgent prescriptions, we offer express delivery within 2–3 hours at a nominal fee.

Are all medicines genuine?

Absolutely. We source all our medicines directly from licensed manufacturers and authorized distributors. Every product is checked for authenticity and expiry date before dispatch. Your health and safety are our top priorities.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and digital wallets. We also offer cash on delivery (COD) for orders within the city limits. All transactions are securely encrypted.
