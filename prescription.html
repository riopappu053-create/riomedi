Guidelines

What makes a Valid Prescription?

To ensure safety and comply with regulations, our pharmacists can only dispense medicines against a valid prescription. Please ensure your upload contains the following details.

  • Doctor Details

    Doctor's name, clinic/hospital name, address, and registration number.

  • Patient Details

    Full name, age, and ideally gender of the patient.

  • Date of Prescription

    Date of consultation. Prescriptions older than specific timeframes may not be valid for certain drugs.

  • Signature

    Digital or physical signature/stamp of the consulting doctor.

Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my prescription has multiple medicines?

Our pharmacist will decode the entire prescription and add all available medicines to your cart. You can then review the cart and choose to buy all or specific items before checking out.

Can I get generic substitutes for prescribed brands?

Yes. If you mention it in the 'Additional Notes' during upload, our pharmacist will suggest FDA-approved generic alternatives with the exact same composition, which can save you up to 40% on costs.

How long does the review process take?

During business hours (8 AM - 10 PM), most prescriptions are reviewed and converted into orders within 15-30 minutes. You will receive an SMS and email notification once your cart is ready.

Is a digital/e-prescription acceptable?

Yes, we fully accept valid e-prescriptions generated by registered teleconsultation platforms or doctors, provided they contain the doctor's registration number and a digital signature.
